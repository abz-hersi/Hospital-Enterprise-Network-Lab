\# Business Requirements



AzizCare Hospital needs a network that is secure, organised and easy to manage.



The main requirements are:



\- Separate departments using VLANs

\- Prevent unnecessary access between departments

\- Keep Guest Wi-Fi away from the internal hospital network

\- Protect Servers, Finance and Medical devices

\- Allow IT to manage network devices

\- Provide Internet access where needed

\- Reduce unnecessary broadcast traffic

\- Avoid single points of failure where possible

\- Make the network easy to troubleshoot and expand



\## Why Segmentation Is Needed



Different departments do not need access to everything.



For example, Doctors should not have direct access to Finance systems, and Guest users should not be able to access any internal hospital resources.



VLANs will be used to separate these groups into different networks, while routing, ACLs and firewall rules will later control what communication is allowed between them.
